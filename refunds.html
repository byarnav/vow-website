Vow comes with a 14-day money-back guarantee. If Vow isn't right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of buying it and you'll get your money back in full. You don't need to give a reason.

Paddle is our merchant of record. Paddle processes the payment and the refund, and the money goes back to the payment method you used. Your bank may take a few days to show it.

Your license key stops working once the refund is processed.
