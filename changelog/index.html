What's new in Vow

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Release notes for Vow, the Mac app that turns off your internet until your timer ends. The newest version is first. Every 1.x update is free for license holders.

Vow checks for updates about once a day and verifies each update with a cryptographic signature before it installs. Update checks never happen while a session is running.

Vow 1.0.18

November 2026

  • Easier install: the download now shows just Vow and your Applications folder, with an arrow telling you where to drag it.
  • Steadier countdown: when your screen dims or wakes during offline time, the countdown stays on screen instead of briefly showing a status message.

Vow 1.0.17

November 2026

  • Simpler first-time setup: a few short steps with a picture of the one switch to turn on, and Vow notices by itself when you're done.
  • Clearer words everywhere: plain, friendly messages instead of technical ones, and easier-to-read text.
  • A shorter, friendlier Help guide that tells you exactly where to click.
  • Schedules and license screens now say clearly what to do next.

Vow 1.0.16

November 2026

  • Quiet login launch: when starting at login, Vow opens discreetly in the menu bar without interrupting your workspace with an open window.
  • Helper update indicator: clear notice in the menu bar if a helper update ever requires attention.

Vow 1.0.15

November 2026

  • Significant idle CPU reduction to ~0.1% while sitting quietly in the background.
  • Full accessibility support: Full Keyboard Access, VoiceOver adjustable controls and spoken announcements, Increase Contrast, and Differentiate Without Color.

Vow 1.0.14

November 2026

  • Smoother license key entry during the free trial.
  • Refreshed interface polish.

Vow 1.0.13

November 2026

  • Automatic checkout in your local currency, with UPI support in India.
  • Faster background updates.

Vow 1.0.12

November 2026

  • Scheduled deep-work sessions.
  • Custom lock durations.

Vow 1.0.12 was the first public release. Earlier versions were internal development builds.
