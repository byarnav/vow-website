What's new in Vow

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Release notes for Vow, the Mac app that turns off your internet until your timer ends. The newest version is first. Every 1.x update is free for license holders.

Vow checks for updates about once a day and verifies each update with a cryptographic signature before it installs. Update checks never happen while a session is running.
