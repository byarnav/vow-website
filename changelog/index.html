What's new in Vow

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Release notes for Vow, the Mac app that turns off your internet until your timer ends. The newest version is first. Every 1.x update is free for license holders.

Vow checks for updates about once a day and verifies each update with a cryptographic signature before it installs. Update checks never happen while a session is running.

Vow 1.0.27

November 2026

  • Fixed the update address so future versions download reliably.

Vow 1.0.26

November 2026

  • Vow now confirms your license key online at least once every 14 days. If it cannot, it asks you to click Check Again.
  • The blue buttons stay blue when the Vow window is behind another window.
  • On a Mac set to Light, the main screen's text and the Hide Window button are easy to read, also right after your offline time ends.
  • The Repair button now works every time you click it.

Vow 1.0.25

November 2026

  • The daily license check is stricter. A key that is not real, or is already used on 2 Macs, no longer keeps Vow unlocked. Being offline never locks Vow.
  • Saved schedules are removed if your license is refunded or no longer valid.
  • The 3 free sessions are now counted once per Mac.

Vow 1.0.24

November 2026

  • The countdown box stays dark and easy to read for your whole offline time.
  • The main screen's text is clear again right after your offline time ends.

Vow 1.0.23

November 2026

  • The countdown box now stays dark for your whole offline time, instead of sometimes turning pale.

Vow 1.0.22

November 2026

  • Smoother start: when you click Go Offline, Vow shows "Going offline…" and then goes straight to the countdown.
  • Easier to read: after your offline time ends, the main screen's text is clear again instead of faint white.
  • Fairer licenses: Vow checks your license now and then while you're online. It never locks you out just because you're offline.

Vow 1.0.21

November 2026

  • Clearer first-time setup: Vow explains why your Mac asks for your password, and that Vow never sees it.
  • Correct time in the menu bar: right after your screen wakes, the menu bar shows the right time left.
  • Friendlier free trial: Vow tells you before your last free session, and says thanks when your free sessions are used.

Vow 1.0.20

November 2026

  • Even smoother updates: right after an update, Vow shows a short "Getting Vow ready" moment instead of an error, and gets ready faster.

Vow 1.0.19

November 2026

  • Smoother updates: after an update, Vow finishes setting itself up within a few seconds, with nothing for you to click.

Vow 1.0.18

November 2026

  • Easier install: the download now shows just Vow and your Applications folder, with an arrow telling you where to drag it.
  • Steadier countdown: when your screen dims or wakes during offline time, the countdown stays on screen instead of briefly showing a status message.

Vow 1.0.17

November 2026

  • Simpler first-time setup: a few short steps with a picture of the one switch to turn on, and Vow notices by itself when you're done.
  • Clearer words everywhere: plain, friendly messages instead of technical ones, and easier-to-read text.
  • A shorter, friendlier Help guide that tells you exactly where to click.
  • Schedules and license screens now say clearly what to do next.

Vow 1.0.16

November 2026

  • Quiet login launch: when starting at login, Vow opens discreetly in the menu bar without interrupting your workspace with an open window.
  • Helper update indicator: clear notice in the menu bar if a helper update ever requires attention.

Vow 1.0.15

November 2026

  • Significant idle CPU reduction to ~0.1% while sitting quietly in the background.
  • Full accessibility support: Full Keyboard Access, VoiceOver adjustable controls and spoken announcements, Increase Contrast, and Differentiate Without Color.

Vow 1.0.14

November 2026

  • Smoother license key entry during the free trial.
  • Refreshed interface polish.

Vow 1.0.13

November 2026

  • Automatic checkout in your local currency, with UPI support in India.
  • Faster background updates.

Vow 1.0.12

November 2026

  • Scheduled deep-work sessions.
  • Custom lock durations.
