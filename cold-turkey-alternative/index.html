Vow vs Cold Turkey: a simpler Cold Turkey alternative for Mac

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Cold Turkey blocks the sites and apps on your lists, using browser extensions and a background agent, on Mac and Windows. Vow turns off your Mac's whole internet connection until a timer ends, with no extensions and nothing to set up. Pick Cold Turkey for detailed control. Pick Vow for total quiet, for $9.99 once.

The short verdict

Choose Cold Turkey if…

  • You also use Windows and want the same blocker on both.
  • You want fine control: specific sites and apps, exceptions, scheduled breaks and allowances.
  • You only need website blocking, which Cold Turkey's free version covers.

Choose Vow if…

  • You want every app offline, not only browsers, without apps being force-quit.
  • You don't want browser extensions that see every URL you visit.
  • You'd rather not maintain lists of sites, apps and exceptions.
  • You want a lower one-time price: $9.99 instead of $45.

How blocking works differently

Cold Turkey is a detailed blocker. Browser extensions check every URL against your lists, and a background agent quits apps you've blocked. Even its block-everything mode (*.*) only applies inside browsers. Messaging apps like Discord or Mail are stopped by force-quitting them, which requires Pro.

Vow works one level lower. It turns off your Mac's network connections using built-in macOS tools, so every app and browser loses its connection at once, and no extension is involved. Your apps stay open, so you can still read old messages and finish a draft offline. Nothing new comes in until the session ends.

Staying locked

Cold Turkey is hard to get around: its background agent respawns if you quit it, and its extensions close browsers if they're disabled. With Pro you can set breaks and delays, or lock a block until it ends.

Vow is just as hard to undo, with one deliberate exception. A system helper holds the timer across quit and restart, and there's no cancel button. If you have a real emergency, you can request an early unlock: your Mac stays offline for 15 more minutes first, and then the internet comes back. You're never trapped.

Price

Cold Turkey's free version blocks websites. Pro, which adds app blocking and more, is $45 once. Vow is $9.99 once for up to 2 personal Macs, with all 1.x updates included and 3 free sessions to try it first.

Switching from Cold Turkey to Vow

  • No lists to move. Vow takes the whole Mac offline.
  • No extensions to install in Safari, Chrome, Firefox or any other browser.
  • Recreate scheduled blocks as Vow schedules, such as Monday to Friday at 18:00 for 1 hour.
  • Developers: you don't need localhost exceptions. Loopback stays up during a session.

Is Vow a good Cold Turkey alternative for Mac?

Yes, if you want your whole Mac offline rather than a list of sites and apps blocked. Vow turns off every network connection until your timer ends, without browser extensions, and costs $9.99 once instead of $45 for Cold Turkey Pro.

Does Vow need browser extensions like Cold Turkey?

No. Vow turns off your Mac's network connections, so it works in every browser automatically and never sees which sites you visit.

Does Vow force-quit my apps?

No. Your apps stay open during a session, so you can read old messages and keep working offline. They just can't connect to the internet until the timer ends.

Can Vow block just some websites?

No. Vow blocks the whole internet. If you want to block specific sites and keep the rest, Cold Turkey or Freedom is the better choice.
