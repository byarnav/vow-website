Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Vow doesn't collect anything about you. No analytics, no tracking, no account. It goes online for two things only: checking for updates, and checking your license key.

What Vow does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No account, email address or sign-up.
  • No advertising, and no sharing or selling of data. We have none to share.
  • It doesn't read your files, your browsing, or what you do on your Mac.

The only two times Vow goes online

1. Checking for updates. Vow asks a file hosted on GitHub whether a newer version exists. As with any web request, GitHub can see your IP address and the app's name and version. Vow doesn't send a profile of your Mac. Updates are checked about once a day, and each one is verified with a cryptographic signature before it installs.

2. Checking your license key. When you enter a key, Vow sends it to our license server at license.byarnav.com to confirm it is valid. The server counts how many Macs a key is activated on, which is how your license covers up to two personal Macs. Vow never does this while an offline session is running.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your license key and trial count are stored in your Mac's Keychain.
  • Your session and schedules are saved in a system folder so the timer survives a restart.
  • A short log of what the helper did (for example, "session started"), kept in /Library/Logs/OfflineLock for troubleshooting. It never leaves your Mac unless you choose to send it to us.

Uninstalling Vow removes the helper. Its Help menu has the steps.

Buying Vow

Payment is handled entirely by Paddle, our merchant of record. We never see your card details. Paddle shares your email and purchase details with us so we can deliver your license key, resend it if you lose it, and give support. Paddle's privacy policy applies to the purchase: paddle.com/legal/privacy.

This website

This site has no analytics of its own. Prices and checkout are provided by Paddle, which may set its own cookies. The free trial download is hosted on GitHub.

Children’s Privacy

Vow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top will change with it. Vow collecting more data is not something we plan to do.
