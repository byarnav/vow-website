Free internet blockers for Mac, and the toughest one

Last updated: November 14, 2026

You can block websites on a Mac for free with Screen Time or SelfControl, and turn off the internet with the Wi-Fi switch. Free tools either block a list of sites or are easy to undo. If you need your whole Mac offline until a timer ends, Vow does that, with 3 free sessions to try.

The quick answer

The best free options are Screen Time (built in, blocks sites you choose), SelfControl (free app, blocks a list of sites until a timer ends) and simply turning off Wi-Fi. None of them can take your whole Mac offline and keep it off until a timer ends. If you need that, Vow does it, with 3 free sessions to try.

Turn off Wi-Fi

Click the Wi-Fi icon in the menu bar and switch it off. It’s instant and free, but it’s one click to turn back on, there’s no timer, and an Ethernet cable or iPhone hotspot still gets you online.

Screen Time

Built into every Mac. Block the sites you choose, or turn on Limit Adult Websites.[1] Steps: how to block websites on a Mac.

SelfControl

A free, open-source Mac app. Add websites to a list, set a timer, and those sites stay blocked until it ends, even if you restart. It only blocks the sites on your list. More: Vow vs SelfControl.

Cold Turkey Blocker (free version)

The free version blocks websites on a timer. Blocking apps, schedules and its locking features need the paid Pro version. More: Vow vs Cold Turkey.

Browser extensions

Free extensions like LeechBlock NG block sites inside one browser. They’re easy to get around: open a different browser.

Family-safe DNS

Cloudflare’s free 1.1.1.3 blocks adult and harmful sites in every browser.[2] Steps: how to block adult sites on a Mac.

What free blockers can’t do

Every free option above has the same weak spot: it blocks a list, or it’s easy to undo. Block Instagram, and you open TikTok. Block TikTok, and you open YouTube. Turn off Wi-Fi, and you turn it back on.
